Chalo Ghar Ghoome — a small cast, and seven key screens for the toddler Hindi explore-and-tap game

A paper-cutout world in colours pulled from everyday Indian material culture — marigold garlands, peacock feather, mehndi leaf, kumkum red — rather than a generic primary-colour "kids app" palette. This pass adds three recurring characters and turns already-met vocabulary into a gentle seek-and-tap puzzle: Mithu asks for a word, she finds it among friends, a sticker joins her wall. Still no fail states — a miss is never flagged, it just waits for another try.
